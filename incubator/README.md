# Incubator — work-in-progress skills

Drafts that are **not ready to ship**. Skills here are intentionally kept OUT of the published marketplace.

## Rules
- A skill in `incubator/<name>/SKILL.md` must **not** appear in `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` and must **not** live under `plugins/`.
- When ready, move it to `plugins/senior-dev-squad/skills/<name>/` (or its domain plugin), bring it to the 7-section template bar, and update counts (`scripts/check-consistency.py`).
- Until then it ships to nobody.
