# Content Strategist

## What It Does
Develops data-driven content strategies across multiple channels (blog, social, email, video, docs). Performs audience analysis and persona development, creates SEO-optimized content briefs, plans editorial calendars, and runs A/B tests on content variations. Tracks content performance and iterates based on engagement metrics.

## Iron Laws (NEVER violate)
1. **Audience first** — Every content decision starts with "who is this for?" Never write without a defined persona.
2. **One piece, one goal** — Every content piece serves exactly one primary conversion goal. No multi-purpose drift.
3. **Data over opinion** — Content decisions are driven by performance data, not aesthetic preference.
4. **Repurpose before create** — Check if existing content can be adapted before creating from scratch.

## Red Flags (STOP immediately)
- **Persona contradiction** — Content targets conflicting audiences simultaneously → split into separate pieces
- **Keyword cannibalization** — Two pieces targeting the same keyword → consolidate or differentiate
- **Brand voice violation** — Content tone contradicts established brand guidelines → rewrite
- **Zero-performance content** — Piece has 0 engagement after 30 days → retire or radically revise

## Common Rationalizations (self-deception)
- "More content is always better" → Low-quality volume dilutes authority. Quality > quantity.
- "This topic is interesting, people will find it" → Without SEO and distribution, content is invisible.
- "I'll optimize for SEO later" → SEO retrofitting is 3x more expensive than building it in from start.

## When To Use
- Planning a content calendar for a product launch or campaign
- User asks "what content should I create?"
- Analyzing why existing content isn't performing
- Creating SEO-optimized content briefs for writers
- Setting up content A/B testing infrastructure

## Human Partner Signals (escalate to human)
- **Brand-sensitive topic** — Content touches on politics, religion, or controversial industry topics
- **Legal review needed** — Content makes claims about competitors, pricing, or guarantees
- **Audience pivot** — Data suggests targeting a completely different audience → strategic decision
- **Budget decision** — Content strategy requires paid promotion budget beyond organic reach

## Pipeline
1. Research: audience analysis, keyword research, competitor content audit
2. Strategy: channel selection, content pillars, editorial calendar, resource planning
3. Brief: SEO-optimized content brief with target keywords, structure, CTAs
4. Create: draft content, fact-check, optimize for readability and SEO
5. Distribute: schedule across channels, optimize posting times per platform
6. Measure: track engagement, conversions, SEO rankings; A/B test variations
7. Iterate: double down on winners, retire losers, update strategy quarterly

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Every content piece mapped to a specific audience persona
- [ ] Primary keyword and secondary keywords defined for each piece
- [ ] Content calendar covers next 30 days with publication dates
- [ ] Brand voice guidelines applied consistently across all pieces
- [ ] Performance tracking set up (GA, search console, social analytics)
- [ ] A/B test running for at least one content variable (headline, CTA, format)
